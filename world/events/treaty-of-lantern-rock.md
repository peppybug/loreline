The treaty was signed in the lamp room of [[Lantern Rock]], on neutral ground kept by the [[Tidewardens]]. The
royalist lords recognised the [[Saltreach Compact]] as the government of the isles. In return, the Compact agreed
that the throne of Vael was vacant, not abolished.

It was a compromise that nobody liked, and it held for almost three hundred years.
