Twenty-two years after the Tide, the captains who had carried the survivors south met in the hull-hall at
[[Merrow]] and signed an agreement. Harbours would be shared. Disputes would be settled by a council. Votes would be
counted by tonnage of shipping. And there would be no king.

That last clause was the point. The captains had watched a queen drown a kingdom with one edict.

The [[Saltreach Compact]] became the government of the isles, and within a century, the richest power on the sea.
