The Compact's council voted, by tonnage, to end the Hollow Court. In Mistmoot of 344 AT its soldiers landed at
[[Thornfield]] and set fire to the town. [[Lysa Marrow]] was killed in the reprisals that followed.

The Court did not end. It went underground, and when it came back, it came back armed.
