When the Synod moved to take the isles in 386 AT, the [[Saltreach Compact]] and the [[Hollow Court]], who had
spent forty years killing each other, found themselves on the same side of the walls of [[Emberkeep]].

The siege lasted three years. It ended when the Keep's warden, [[Sefa Renn]], opened the gates to let in refugees
from the eruption, and refused to close them again.
