On the fifteenth of Seedtide, 412 AT, the anniversary of the first dawn after the Tide, [[Edran Vael]] was crowned
in the hull-hall at [[Merrow]]. The crown was new. The old one is still at the bottom of the sea, with
[[Queen Isembard]].

The Compact chose him. The Synod blessed him. The Hollow Court, which still meets around an empty chair, sent no one.

Whether this ends the arguments of the last four centuries or begins a new one is, at the time of writing, unclear.
