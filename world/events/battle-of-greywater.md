[[Corwen Harrow]] had gathered sixty ships and a claim to the throne of Vael. He meant to sail into [[Merrow]] and
take the crown back. The Compact had forty ships, and the [[Saltwind Charts]].

Admiral [[Tamsin Ord]] let the Claimant's fleet into [[Greywater Sound]], then took her own ships through the deep
channel behind it. At low tide the royalist fleet found itself trapped on the sandbars with the enemy behind it.

Corwen was killed. Most of his captains surrendered by nightfall.
