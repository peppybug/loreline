On the ninth of Emberfall, the mountain on [[Cinder Isle]] that the [[Ember Synod]] called the First Flame woke up.
The eruption went on, on and off, for eight months. Ash fell across all the isles, and that summer's harvest failed.

The Synod took it as a sign. If the sea had been the first judgement, fire was the second, and the Synod would be
its instrument. The Ember Years had begun.
