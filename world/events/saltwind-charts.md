[[Mira Saltwind]] sailed south in 57 AT with one ship and a crew of nine, and came back fourteen years later with a set
of charts that showed every reef, current and anchorage between the isles and the southern continent.

The charts made the [[Saltreach Compact]] rich almost overnight. They also included, on sheet eleven, the one deep
channel through the shallows of [[Greywater Sound]], which the Compact immediately classified as a state secret.
