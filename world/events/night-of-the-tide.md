On the fourteenth of Seedtide, a spring tide and a storm out of the north-west arrived together. The western
sea-gates were open, as the [[Edict of the Open Gates]] required. They could not be closed against the water
pouring through them.

The sea scoured out the ground behind the gates, and the [[Long Wall]] began to fall in on itself. By midnight it was
breached in eleven places. By morning the lowlands of Vael, and [[Vaelhold]] with them, were under the sea.

[[Queen Isembard]] was last seen on the palace steps, directing boats to the lower city.

## The year that began everything

The islanders count their years from this night. Year 1 After the Tide began at dawn on the fifteenth of Seedtide,
and every calendar in the isles still starts there.
