[[High Flame Aurel]] declared in 212 AT that the [[Ember Synod]] should govern the isles directly, as the only power
that had foreseen the Tide. Half the Synod agreed. The other half said that a faith which ruled would soon stop
believing in anything but itself.

After seven years of rival councils and one burned library, Aurel's party held [[Emberkeep]] and the dissenters were
expelled. They went to the island towns and preached in the streets.
