The survivors of the Tide had nowhere to go but south, to the islands that had been Vael's poorest province. They
came in fishing boats, barges, and in one famous case a brewery vat.

There was not enough land, so they lived on the ships. At [[Merrow]], the largest harbour, the first fleet was
dragged ashore and roofed over, and the hulls became the first streets.

It took six years before the last refugee camp on the shore was cleared.
