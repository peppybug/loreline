The edict was simple: on the highest tides of each month, the sea-gates of the [[Long Wall]] would be opened for
one hour, under guard, to let seawater into the [[Saltpans]].

The [[Tidewardens]] refused. Their Master read Hallam's first rule aloud in the throne room. Isembard had him
replaced, and the gates opened in Greening of 3 BT.

For two years it worked. The pans filled, salt came back to the markets, and the famine ended. People named their
daughters after the queen.
