For as long as anyone remembered, the northern coast had belonged to five quarrelling chiefdoms, each with its own
harbour and its own salt. In 612 BT their chiefs met on the hill that would become [[Vaelhold]] and swore to a
single war-leader, Aldric, whom the songs call the First Crown.

The union was practical before it was anything else. The coast was sinking, slowly, and the floods were getting
worse. No chiefdom could hold back the sea alone.

## What it changed

The founding gave the north one law, one coin and, within three centuries, one wall. It also gave it a royal line,
the [[House of Vael]], whose memory has outlived its kingdom by four hundred years.
