By the last decades of the Age of Crowns, the [[Saltpans]] behind the Wall had been drying for a century. In 14 BT
they failed almost completely. Without salt there was nothing to trade and, worse, no way to cure fish or meat for
the winter.

Two hard winters killed thousands. A third, in 9 BT, emptied whole villages. [[Queen Isembard]], who came to the
throne in the middle of it, ate what the palace kitchens fed the city, and ordered the royal treasury sold for grain.
It was not enough.

## Why it matters

The famine is the first link in the chain that ended the kingdom. It is also why many islanders remember Isembard
kindly, even after what came next.
