[[Hallam the Mason]] proposed the Wall to King Teodor III as a way to end the floods for good. It took sixteen
years, a tenth of the kingdom's men and most of its treasury. When it was finished it ran from the western cliffs to
[[Lantern Rock]], with great timber sea-gates every few miles to let the rivers out.

Hallam founded the [[Tidewardens]] to keep it, and wrote the rules they swore to. The first rule was that the gates
were never to be opened on a spring tide.

## The cost

The Wall saved the lowlands from the sea, and in doing so slowly starved them of it. The saltpans that had made
Vael rich needed seawater, and now they only got what the Wardens let through.
