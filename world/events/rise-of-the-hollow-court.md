By 300 AT, the [[Saltreach Compact]] was governed by a few dozen shipping families, and the islands' poor had
nothing to vote with. The heirs of the Synod's exiled preachers had been telling them for eighty years that no
throne was holy. [[Lysa Marrow]] told them that no throne was necessary.

The [[Hollow Court]] met in empty warehouses and abandoned halls, always around an empty chair. Its first
stronghold was the farming town of [[Thornfield]].
