[[Oren Tallow]] was thirty-three when a fever nearly killed him. When he recovered, he walked up to the hot springs
above his village and stayed there for a month. He came down with a message: the world was made in fire, the sea was
its enemy, and one day the sea would come over the Wall and drown the proud.

He gathered followers in the caves that later became [[Emberkeep]]. The Vaelish court thought him harmless. For
eighty-eight years, he was.
