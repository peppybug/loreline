kind: Sea wall

map: [50, 30]
aliases: [Hallam's Wall]
---

Ninety miles of stone and earth that held the sea back from the Vaelish lowlands for three centuries. Built by [[Hallam the Mason]] and kept by the [[Tidewardens]]. Its broken top still shows above the water at low tide.
