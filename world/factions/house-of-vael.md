The royal house that ruled the northern coast for six centuries, from [[Founding of Vael|its founding]] to the
[[Night of the Tide]]. Its kings governed from [[Vaelhold]] and kept the sea out with the [[Long Wall]].

The house ended with [[Queen Isembard]], who went down with her capital. Nearly every faction since has claimed
to be its rightful heir, which is most of the reason the Saltreach Isles have so rarely been at peace.
