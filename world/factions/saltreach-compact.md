A league of island captains and harbour-masters that turned a refugee fleet into a trading power. The Compact has
no king. It is governed by a council that sits in [[Merrow]] and votes by tonnage.

Its rise is told in [[Founding of the Saltreach Compact]]. Its long rivalry with the would-be heirs of Vael came to
a head at the [[Battle of Greywater]].
