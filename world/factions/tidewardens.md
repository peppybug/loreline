The masons and watchmen who kept the [[Long Wall]]. In the Age of Crowns they were the most respected order in
Vael. After the Wall failed they were blamed for the [[Night of the Tide]], and the survivors scattered.

A remnant still keeps the beacon at [[Lantern Rock]], and still insists that the Wall did not fail. It was opened.
