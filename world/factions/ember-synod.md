An order of fire-priests founded by [[Oren Tallow]] in the highlands of the Emberwild. The Synod teaches that the
world was made in fire and will be remade in it, and that the sea is the enemy of everything living.

After the [[Night of the Tide]] that teaching suddenly looked like prophecy, and the Synod grew from a hill cult
into the most powerful institution in the isles. It split in two during the [[Synod Schism]].
