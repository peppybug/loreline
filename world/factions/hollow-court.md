A movement of the poor and the dispossessed that grew up in the shadow of the Compact's wealth. Its members hold
court in empty halls, around an empty throne, and say that no one should sit there again.

Led for most of its history by [[Lysa Marrow]], the Court was hunted after the [[Burning of Thornfield]], and
became one of the three powers at the [[Siege of Emberkeep]].
