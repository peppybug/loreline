A charcoal-burner's son from the Emberwild who, after a fever, began to preach that the sea would one day rise and
drown the proud. He founded the [[Ember Synod]] in a cave above the hot springs at [[Emberkeep]].

He died seven decades before the [[Night of the Tide]]. His followers have never stopped pointing that out.
