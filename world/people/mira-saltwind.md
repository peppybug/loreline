Born on a refugee ship in the first years after the Tide, Mira grew up on the water and spent her life mapping it.
Her [[Saltwind Charts]] opened the sea lanes south, and made the [[Saltreach Compact]] rich.

She refused a seat on the Compact's council three times. The fourth time, they gave it to her ship.
