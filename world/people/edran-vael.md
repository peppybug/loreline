A ship's clerk with a disputed claim to the old royal name, chosen by the [[Saltreach Compact]] to end the wars of
the Ember Years. He was crowned at [[Merrow]] in 412 AT, the first king anywhere in four centuries.

Whether he is a king or a figurehead is the main subject of conversation in every harbour tavern in the isles.
