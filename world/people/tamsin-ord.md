The Compact's admiral at the [[Battle of Greywater]], who used the [[Saltwind Charts]] to bring her ships through
the shallows behind [[Corwen Harrow|the Claimant's]] fleet. She negotiated the [[Treaty of Lantern Rock]] and is
remembered as the founder of the Compact's navy.
