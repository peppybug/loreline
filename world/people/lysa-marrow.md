A net-maker from the poor quarter of [[Merrow]] who became the first Voice of the [[Hollow Court]]. Her speeches,
copied by hand and passed between ships, argued that the isles had spent three hundred years mourning a crown that
had only ever fed itself.

She was killed in the reprisals after the [[Burning of Thornfield]]. The Court elects a new Voice every year in
her name.
