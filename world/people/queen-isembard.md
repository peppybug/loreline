The last ruler of the [[House of Vael]]. Isembard came to the throne young, in the middle of the [[Salt Famine]],
and spent her reign trying to feed a kingdom that the land could no longer support.

Her answer was the [[Edict of the Open Gates]]: let the sea into the salt pans behind the Wall, on the high tides,
under watch. It worked for two years. On the [[Night of the Tide]] it did not, and Isembard refused to leave
[[Vaelhold]] while there were still people in the lower city. She was never seen again.

Islanders still call her the Drowned Queen. Whether that is a title of mourning or of blame depends on who you ask.
